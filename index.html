The Evolution of Internet Memes

A Mini Weblog

Memes have become one of the most powerful forms of online communication. From image macros to modern TikTok trends, memes shape humor, culture, and even world events. This page explains where memes came from, how they changed, and why they spread so quickly.

Major Stages of Meme History

  • Image Macro(2007–2012)
  • iconic reaction images (2016–2019)
  • Video memes & TikTok formats (2019–present)
