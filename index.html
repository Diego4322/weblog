Welcome to THE MEME CATALOG

The first meme is often considered to be “Kilroy Was Here, a doodle that spread during World War II long before the internet existed. It showed a bald man with a big nose peeking over a wall, with the phrase “Kilroy Was Here” written underneath. Soldiers drew it on walls, ships, and equipment all over the world, and it became a global inside joke everyone recognized it, but no one knew exactly where it started. This early meme worked the same way modern memes do: it spread person to person, changed slightly each time, and created a shared sense of humor and connection. While today’s memes move digitally instead of through graffiti, “Kilroy Was Here” shows that meme culture has been around far longer than the internet.
